Here are simple and casual questions you can ask her to get to know her better. ❤️

### **About Her**

1. What's your favorite color?
2. What's your favorite food?
3. What's your favorite drink?
4. What's your favorite movie?
5. What's your favorite song?
6. What's your favorite hobby?
7. What's your favorite place?
8. Are you a morning or night person?
9. What's your biggest pet peeve?
10. What's your favorite thing to do?

### **Her Personality**

11. How would you describe yourself?
12. Are you introverted or extroverted?
13. Are you more emotional or logical?
14. What makes you happy?
15. What makes you sad?
16. What easily annoys you?
17. What's your biggest fear?
18. What's your best quality?
19. What's one thing you dislike about yourself?
20. What makes you feel appreciated?

### **Her Preferences**

21. Do you prefer calls or chats?
22. Do you prefer staying home or going out?
23. Do you prefer beaches or mountains?
24. Do you like surprises?
25. Do you prefer sweet or salty food?
26. Do you like cats or dogs?
27. What's your ideal weekend?
28. What's your comfort food?
29. What's your favorite childhood memory?
30. What's your dream destination?

### **A Little More Personal**

31. What's your biggest dream?
32. What's something you want to achieve?
33. What's something people often misunderstand about you?
34. What's your love language?
35. How do you handle stress?
36. What makes you feel safe?
37. What's something you've always wanted to try?
38. What's one thing you wish people knew about you?
39. What's your happiest memory?
40. What's something that always makes you smile?

**Tip:** Ask one question at a time so it feels like a natural conversation, not an interview. ❤️
